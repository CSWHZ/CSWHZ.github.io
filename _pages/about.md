About Me
======
Hi, my name is Hanzhang Wang (王含章). I am a first-year Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at City University of Hong Kong (CityUHK), advised by [Ziye Ma](https://gavenma.github.io/). Before joining CityUHK, I graduated from Harbin Institute of Technology, Shenzhen. I always welcome opportunities for collaboration, so please feel free to reach out at <hanzwang6-c@my.cityu.edu.hk>.

Research Interests
======
My research interests focus on:
* **LLM Pretraining:** Improving pretraining itself and enhancing generalization to downstream tasks.
* **Efficient AI:** Developing efficient methods for LLM, with a focus on model quantization and zeroth-order optimization algorithms.
